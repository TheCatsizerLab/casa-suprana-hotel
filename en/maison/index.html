A coastal house · Cap Corse

Quite simply, a house.

Casa Suprana imagines hospitality on a human scale: eighteen keys, a Mediterranean garden and the sea as the only appointment that matters.

Quite simply, a house.

In the morning, shutters open to the light of Cap Corse. You wander down for coffee, find a place in the shade, return to a book left the evening before. Nothing dictates the day. Each space is imagined so that ordinary moments become the ones you take home.

Thoughtful details. Effortless gestures.

Natural materials, washed linen, pale wood and colours borrowed from the landscape. The garden uses little water; bathroom amenities are refillable. These are design choices for this fictional hotel, with no environmental certification claimed.
